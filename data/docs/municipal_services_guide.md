# Reporting faults

Water outages, electricity faults, burst pipes, sewage overflows, uncollected refuse and potholes are reported to your local municipality, usually through its call centre, website or app. Record the reference number you are given when you log a fault. If a fault is an emergency, such as a major pipe burst or exposed electrical wiring, use the municipality's emergency number.

## Escalating an unresolved fault

If a fault is not attended to after a reasonable time, quote your original reference number and ask for the matter to be escalated to the relevant department head or ward councillor. Keep a record of dates, reference numbers and the names of the officials you spoke to.

## Querying a municipal account

If your water, electricity or rates bill appears incorrect, lodge a billing query with the municipality's revenue or customer services office and ask for a meter reading check. You may be allowed to withhold the disputed portion while the query is investigated, but confirm this with the municipality because rules vary. Keep paying the undisputed amount.
