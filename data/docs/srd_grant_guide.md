# Applying for the SRD grant

Applications for the Social Relief of Distress grant are made through the official SASSA channels, such as the SASSA SRD website. Applicants normally provide their identity number and a cellphone number that belongs to them. Only use official SASSA channels and never pay anyone to apply on your behalf.

## Checking your application status

Applicants can check the status of an application on the official SASSA SRD status page using their identity number and cellphone number. The status shows whether an application is pending, approved or declined, and the reason where one is given.

## If an application is declined

If an application is declined, read the reason shown on the status page. In some cases an applicant can lodge an appeal through the official appeals process within the allowed period. The appeal is reviewed by an independent body. Check the official SASSA website for the current appeal steps and time limit.

## Payments

Approved applicants are paid by the method they chose, for example through a bank account or a retail pay-point. If a payment has not arrived, check your status first and then contact SASSA through its official channels.

## Eligibility

GovAssist cannot decide whether a person qualifies. Eligibility is determined by SASSA using the criteria in force at the time, including checks against other government databases.
