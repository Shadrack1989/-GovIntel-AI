# Registering a company

New companies are registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) through its online registration service. Applicants usually reserve a company name, then submit the registration with the details of the directors and shareholders. A registration certificate is issued when the application is approved.

## Beneficial ownership

Companies are generally required to keep and file information about their beneficial owners. Check the CIPC website for the current filing obligations and deadlines.

## Annual returns

Registered companies must submit an annual return every year after incorporation, and a fee may apply. Failure to file can lead to deregistration. Annual returns are submitted through the CIPC online services. Keep your company details, such as directors and registered address, up to date.

## Business tax registration

A separate tax registration with SARS is needed for company income tax and other tax types. This is handled through the SARS channels.
