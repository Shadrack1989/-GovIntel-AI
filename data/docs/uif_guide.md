# Claiming UIF benefits

Workers who lose their job may be able to claim unemployment benefits from the Unemployment Insurance Fund. Claims are made through the official UIF channels, such as the uFiling online service or a Department of Employment and Labour office. A claim should be lodged soon after employment ends because time limits apply.

## Documents usually requested

Claimants commonly provide their identity document, the UI-19 form completed by the employer, proof of banking details and a termination letter or similar proof of how employment ended. Confirm the current list with UIF.

## If your employer has not paid contributions

Employers are required to declare and pay UIF contributions for their employees. If you suspect your employer has not done so, report it to the Department of Employment and Labour so that it can be investigated. Your claim can be affected, so ask UIF how to proceed.

## Decisions

UIF decides whether a claim is approved and the benefit amount. GovAssist cannot tell you whether you qualify.
