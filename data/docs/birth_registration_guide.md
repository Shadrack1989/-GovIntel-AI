# Registering a birth

A birth should be registered as soon as possible after the baby is born. Registration is normally done at a Home Affairs office, and some health facilities that deliver babies are linked to Home Affairs and can start the registration process.

## What parents usually need

Parents typically bring their identity documents and the notification of birth issued by the clinic or hospital. Where a parent is not a South African citizen, additional documents may be requested. Check the official website or ask the office before travelling.

## Late registration of birth

If a birth was not registered within the standard period, a late registration process applies. This usually requires extra supporting evidence, such as clinic cards or sworn statements, and an interview. Contact Home Affairs to confirm which documents are accepted in your situation.

## Birth certificates

A birth certificate is issued after registration. An unabridged certificate shows the details of the parents and is often required for passport applications and school admissions.
