# Registering for SARS eFiling

Taxpayers who already have a tax reference number can register for eFiling on the SARS website or through the SARS MobiApp. Registration requires a valid email address and cellphone number so that one-time PINs can be sent. Keep your login details private and never share your one-time PIN.

## Submitting an income tax return

Many individual taxpayers receive an auto-assessment, which they can review, accept or edit. Taxpayers who are not auto-assessed submit a return on eFiling. Gather supporting documents such as tax certificates (IRP5), medical aid certificates and retirement annuity certificates before you start.

## Forgotten password or login problems

If you cannot log in to eFiling, use the password reset option on the login page. If your contact details have changed, SARS may need to verify your identity before updating them, which can require a visit to a branch or a verification process. Contact SARS through its official channels.

## Refunds

When a return results in a refund, SARS pays it into the verified bank account on your profile after assessment. Banking details must be verified. Delays can occur if SARS selects a return for verification. Check the status on eFiling and contact SARS if it takes longer than expected.
