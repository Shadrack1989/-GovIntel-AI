# Applying for a Smart ID card

A first-time applicant or someone replacing an ID book usually applies in person at a Home Affairs office that offers Smart ID services, or at a participating bank branch. Applicants must go in person because fingerprints and a photograph are captured at the counter.

## Documents to bring

Applicants typically bring their existing green ID book or, for a first application, a birth certificate and proof of identity from a parent or guardian. Check the official website for the current list, because requirements differ between first applications, replacements and applicants who are minors.

## Lost or stolen ID

If an ID document is lost or stolen, report the loss and apply for a replacement as soon as possible. A police case number or affidavit may be requested. Bring any other identity documents you still have, such as a driving licence or passport.

## After applying

After the application is captured, a receipt is issued. Applicants are usually notified by SMS when the card is ready for collection. Keep the receipt safe, as it is needed for collection. Processing times can change, so ask the office for the current estimate.
