# Renewing a driving licence card

A driving licence card must be renewed before it expires. Renewals are done in person at a Driving Licence Testing Centre (DLTC) or another designated licensing office. Many centres require a booking, which is often made online through the official booking channel.

## What to bring

Applicants usually bring their identity document, the current licence card, and proof of an eye test completed at an optometrist or approved facility. Photographs and fingerprints are captured at the centre. Some centres request passport-style photographs or proof of address, so confirm the list when booking.

## Fees and timing

Fees are set by the licensing authority and can change. Ask the centre for the current fee. After renewal, the new card is normally printed and delivered to the centre or to a designated address; the centre can tell you the usual waiting period. A temporary licence may be issued in the meantime.
